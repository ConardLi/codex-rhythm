# Code of Conduct

Codex Rhythm is committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, respect differing experience levels, and avoid publishing private information. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or deliberately disruptive contributions and may restrict participation when necessary.

To report conduct concerns privately, contact the maintainers through the repository's private security or maintainer contact channel. Reports will be reviewed in confidence and handled proportionately.
